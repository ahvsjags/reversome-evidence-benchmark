# Revision evidence status — 2026-11-09

This append-only status note supplements the existing compact public evidence release. It reports the available, independently checked outputs and their limits; it is not a claim that every revision gate or the complete manuscript archive has been closed.

## External public cohorts

- **GSE319044 (lung subset):** all 9 eligible donors had positive paired-minus-permuted contrasts; mean donor contrast 0.04225; donor-bootstrap 95% CI 0.01650–0.07500; exact sign-test (P=0.00390625). The analysis is restricted to lung samples from the accession.
- **GSE307326:** 16 profiles nested within 8 donors; donor-averaged mean contrast −0.000305934; donor-bootstrap 95% CI −0.000987074 to 0.000148597; exact sign-flip (P=0.5546875). This cohort does not show a positive aggregate transport effect.
- **GSE281482:** four public BAL mesenchymal matrices; 3/4 matrix-level contrasts positive; equal-matrix mean 0.03012735; descriptive matrix-resampling interval −0.00195761 to 0.05488508. The interval is descriptive and does not assume donor independence. The frozen planner withholds the broader all-four-positive claim.
- Official source records: [GSE319044](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE319044), [GSE307326](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE307326), [GSE281482](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE281482).
