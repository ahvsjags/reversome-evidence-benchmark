# v1.0.0-manuscript

First public manuscript-linked REVERSOME release.

This release freezes:

- the specimen-held-out lung RNA-to-ATAC primary analysis;
- formal specimen bootstrap intervals and exact sign-flip tests;
- the four-model embedding benchmark with donor-grouped primary endpoint;
- explicit handling of single-class and fewer-than-50-row dataset folds;
- majority, study-only, expression PCA50-logistic and Ridge baselines;
- full out-of-fold predictions, fold manifests and leakage audit;
- the pre-specified negative PBMC transportability analysis;
- environment locks, licences, provenance records and checksums.

The release contains derived computational artefacts and does not redistribute
third-party model weights or large raw public datasets. It makes no claim of
experimental, causal or clinical validation.
